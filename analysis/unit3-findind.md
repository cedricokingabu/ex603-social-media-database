**Memo to the Dashboard Team**

The dashboard statement mentioned that about half of all reactions failed. However, the data shows that only **11 out of 200 reactions (5.5%) were removed for spam**. In total, 34 reactions (17%) were removed for all reasons combined. These numbers do not support the statement that about half of all reactions failed because of spam.

We found two possible counting problems: reactions with a blank removal reason could be left out, and reactions removed for other reasons could be wrongly counted as spam. We did not receive the dashboard’s original query, so we cannot say what caused the error. The team should check that query against the agreed definition of a spam failure.

The payout should not stay on hold based on this disputed number alone. Recalculate it using a checked query and the agreed definition, then release or adjust the payout based on the correct result.
